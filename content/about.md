I'm a physicist based in the San Francisco Bay Area, working on high energy density physics and pulsed power systems at Fisica ATI. On the side, I consult on quantum computing hardware through my company, SQUID Consultants LLC.

I grew up in western Pennsylvania, where a love of the outdoors ran deep — I'm an Eagle Scout, and I still get outside every chance I get. I earned my PhD in physics from Carnegie Mellon University and completed a postdoc at Sandia National Laboratories before heading west. This site chronicles my publicly available research, projects, and interesting measurements along the way.
