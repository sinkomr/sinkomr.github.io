Hi, I'm Michael Sinko. I'm a physicist living in the Bay Area, and this is my corner of the internet.

I grew up in western Pennsylvania, where I spent most of my time either outside or buried in a book. Scouting was a big part of my childhood, and I stuck with it all the way to Eagle Scout.

For undergrad I went to Miami University to study physics, picking up a BS and MS along the way. My first year there I set up a PCB prototyping line, and after that I spent my lab time characterizing magnetic materials like nanoparticles and thin films. Outside the lab I played intramural broomball and hockey.

Grad school took me to Carnegie Mellon for a PhD in physics, working on 2D materials and superconducting circuits. In practice that meant a whole lot of hours in cleanrooms and nanofab facilities. Along the way I adopted two cats, Pico and Nano, and learned to ski.

After that I headed west to California for a postdoc at Sandia National Laboratories in Livermore, where I set up a nanoimprint lithography system and did UHV work on nickelate superconductors. From there I went to Bleximo doing quantum device fabrication, and when that adventure wound down I started at Fisica ATI, where I now work on high energy density physics and pulsed power systems. I also do quantum computing hardware consulting through my company, SQUID Consultants LLC.

When I'm not doing physics, you'll find me woodworking, 3D printing, or out in the garden.
